Samit Shahnawaz Miftah is a PhD researcher in Computer and Systems Engineering at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI), Troy, New York, where his work sits at the intersection of hardware security, hardware verification, and AI-assisted design automation. He received his Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), and previously pursued his doctoral studies in Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at Dallas (GPA: 3.89/4.00) before transferring to RPI to continue his research in August 2025.

Samit's research focuses on developing automated methodologies for the security verification of hardware designs, with an emphasis on fuzzing-based testing, formal verification, and the integration of large language models (LLMs) into the hardware verification workflow. His work spans symbolic execution-guided hardware fuzzing (SymbFuzz), Network-on-Chip (NoC) verification (InterConfuzz), RTL-level security bug localization, and LLM-driven SystemVerilog assertion generation. He has authored and co-authored 10 peer-reviewed publications — 7 conference papers and 3 journal articles — appearing in premier venues including IEEE MICRO, ACM/IEEE DAC, IEEE HOST, IEEE TCAD, IEEE TVLSI, ACM TODAES, and IEEE VTS.

Beyond his research contributions, Samit is an accomplished competitor in hardware security challenges. He earned second place at Hack@DAC 2025 and third place at Hack@DAC 2024 — among the most prestigious hardware security competitions in the world — and claimed third place in the AI Hardware Attack Challenge at CSAW 2025, having previously been a finalist at CSAW 2022 and CSAW 2023. His technical toolkit spans SystemVerilog, Verilog, Chisel, C/C++, Python, and a wide range of industry-standard EDA tools from Cadence, Synopsys, and Xilinx, alongside formal verification frameworks and LLM integration through LangChain. Samit is deeply committed to advancing the security and reliability of next-generation hardware systems through rigorous, automated, and intelligent verification methodologies.
